This page describes the information used by this educational website and its optional Google sign-in.

Public learning pages

The crop library, practice guides and agriculture notes are public. This static website does not ask for personal information to read them.

Google sign-in

If Firebase Google authentication is configured and you choose to sign in, Google and Firebase process your sign-in. The app can receive your Google account’s display name, email address and profile image. Firebase may retain your sign-in state in this browser so you stay signed in. This static version does not send account details to a separate student database.

Sign-in confirms control of a Google account; it does not verify school enrollment or restrict access to the public pages. Do not use this demo for sensitive student records. For account deletion or questions about Google’s processing, use your Google Account settings and the relevant Google/Firebase privacy information.

Hosting and external links

When hosted online, the hosting provider may process standard technical request data. Links to government, ICAR, Google or other external sites are governed by those sites’ own privacy policies. This local development site is served from your computer.

Before public deployment, replace this notice with the site owner’s contact details, hosting-specific practices and any applicable school, regional and legal requirements.
